LUMA Space and LUMA Stage Have Closed

As of May 25, 2026, four services remained: LUMA Voice, Frame, Path and Circle. LUMA Craft was added on September 26, so there are now five.

The two services that have closed

LUMA Space, which offered counselling, and LUMA Stage, which taught singing, have both closed.

The four that remain share one purpose: helping someone believe in themselves. Rather than add more, I chose to make each of the four deeper.

Enquiries about the closed services

If you write in about either of the closed services, I will let you know they are no longer offered.
