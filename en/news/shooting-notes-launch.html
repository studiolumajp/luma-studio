Shooting Notes Is Now Open

Seven articles on photographs taken at Haneda, Itami, Keihinjima, Shonan, Atami and Yokohama, each with the time of day and the settings used.

The gallery shows the results. These notes are for what was going through my head while shooting.

Seven articles, 180 photographs in total. Haneda by day and by night, Itami Skypark, Keihinjima Tsubasa Park, Shonan and Enoshima, Atami, and Yokohama Minatomirai.

The times at which I changed shutter speed or ISO are written down as they happened. Shoot the same place with different settings and the picture changes, and the notes let you follow that.
