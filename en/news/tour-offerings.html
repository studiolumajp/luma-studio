Tailored LUMA Path tours now have their own page

A new page shows how I plan a day around dietary needs, young children, older guests or short walking distances.

Tours planned around your group

The tailored tours page is for groups that need a little more planning. That could be coeliac disease, a food allergy or another dietary need. It could also be young children who'll need breaks, an older parent who'd like a gentler pace, or someone who finds long walks and stairs hard.

The page sets out the five steps I took for a gluten-free food walk in Tokyo on 8 November 2026. They run from asking about dietary needs before the tour to writing a guide for the guests' next stops. I check each restaurant's official information before the day and ask the staff how a dish is prepared when we get there, but I can't guarantee there's no cross-contamination in any kitchen. What you eat is always your decision.

A page for each area

Each of the 16 areas I guide has two pages. The area page covers the main sights, my notes on a few of them and common questions, with sample routes for each tour length. The tour page gives the meeting point, what's included and what isn't, and what to know before you book.

Fourteen areas offer a half day, a full day or two days, and Kansai's three airports offer a half day or a full day. Mt Fuji is set up as three routes instead. Each is a full day of 7 to 8 hours: Lake Kawaguchi and Fujiyoshida, Mishima and Gotemba, or a shopping day at Gotemba Premium Outlets. For the outlets day, we meet at Busta Shinjuku and ride the bus down together.

Quotes in six currencies

Quotes come in USD, CAD, AUD, NZD, GBP or EUR, so you don't have to convert anything yourself. Any other currency is quoted in USD. One quote covers your whole group of up to six, and it's free.
