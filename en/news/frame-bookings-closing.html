LUMA Frame Stops Taking New Photo Bookings on 30 September

The photographs and shooting notes stay online. LUMA Voice now has a three-month English coaching course.

New photo bookings

LUMA Frame stops taking new photo bookings on 30 September 2026. Shoots that are already booked will go ahead exactly as agreed, including delivery times.

From October 2026, photography requests are handled by a partner photographer. The photographs and the shooting notes stay online.

English coaching and the home page

LUMA Voice now has a three-month English coaching course (¥165,000, tax incl.) for people who use English at work. The home page now opens with S.League, the health-mahjong tournament I have hosted since 2022.
