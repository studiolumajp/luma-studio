The Home Page Has Been Redesigned

S.League match footage and the next tournament now come first, and you can choose where to go by what you want to do.

What you see first

The top of the home page now plays footage from S.League 24, filmed on 25 July 2026. Below it sit the record so far, 24 events and 367 entries since 2022, and the date, fee and entry deadline of the next tournament.

Four ways in

Under that are four entry points, chosen by what you want to do: play in a mahjong tournament, learn to speak English, tour Tokyo in English, or have an event run for you. Pick one to see the key facts and how to book.

The S.League description now matches who actually plays. Professional players and aspiring pros take part, and players at intermediate level and above come to test their game.

The rotating set of fifteen photographs, added on 3 September, has been retired. The photography stays online on the location pages.
