Affiliate links for Saily and NordVPN

I've added a guide to staying connected in Japan, with affiliate links to Saily and NordVPN.

A guide to getting online in Japan

The new guide to staying connected in Japan introduces two products. Saily is an eSIM you use for mobile data, and NordVPN is a VPN for public Wi-Fi.

Saily comes from the company that made NordVPN. You set up the eSIM in the Saily app before you leave, and your plan starts automatically when you arrive. Calls and texts (SMS) stay on your usual number. Use code LUMADESIGN at checkout for 10% off.

NordVPN encrypts your internet traffic and hides your IP address. It's useful whenever you join public Wi-Fi at a hotel, a station or a café, in Japan or back home. NordVPN's Black Friday sale runs until 9 December 2026 and adds 3 extra months to a plan.

Where you'll see them

The LUMA Path page has a short section on both. The LUMA Frame page has a brief note that links to the guide.

How the links work

These are affiliate links. If you buy through them, LUMA earns a commission, at no extra cost to you. Every page or section with these links is labelled as affiliate links. Section 8 of the Disclaimer on the Legal & Policies page sets this out.
