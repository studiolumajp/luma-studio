The Home Page Now Cycles Through Fifteen Photographs

Fifteen photographs from Haneda, Yokohama, Shonan and Sapporo, changing every seven seconds.

Why one photograph became fifteen

Until now a single photograph from Yokohama sat behind the headline. One frame cannot show the range of what LUMA Frame shoots.

The order changes on every page load. A pause button sits in the lower left for anyone who would rather it held still.

158KB, measured

Each photograph has a lighter version made for this. The page loads the one on screen and the one coming next, not all fifteen. Measured, that is 158KB.
