Twenty-Eight Frames at Itami Skypark

From 17:03 to 19:44. Two hours and forty-one minutes, with ISO climbing from 100 to 800.

Shot from a rise level with the runway

Itami Skypark sits alongside Osaka International Airport. A rise in the park puts you level with the runway, facing the aircraft head on as they leave.

The first frame was 1/1250s at ISO400. The last was 3 seconds at ISO800. As the light went, the settings travelled from one end of the range to the other.
