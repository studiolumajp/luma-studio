Overview

The photos you'll still want in ten years.

Natural light,
and nothing staged.

I shoot with the Nikon Z6III and work with whatever light is already there. Pre-weddings, okuizome, family portraits, aircraft and landscapes. Photos come back within 7 days, and you can look at the shots on the back of the camera as we go. I post work on Instagram at @luma_frame.jp.

What's Included

Eleven kinds of session

Eleven categories in all. I plan each one around the place, the time of day and who's in front of the camera.

01

Family

ファミリー

Birthdays, the first day of school, a day out with three generations. Kids change fast, so this is your family exactly as it is this year.

02

Maternity & Baby

マタニティー・赤ちゃん

From the last months of pregnancy through newborn, okuizome and the 100-day celebration. I can come to your home for these.

03

Omiyamairi & Shichi-Go-San

お宮参り・七五三

I come along to the shrine visit and shoot it as it happens, framing wide enough that everyone gets into the shot.

04

Coming-of-Age & Graduation

成人式・卒業袴

Furisode and hakama photographed out in the city or a park rather than in a studio. Dressing and hair/make-up aren't included.

05

Couple & Anniversary

カップル・記念日

An anniversary, or the week after a proposal. I wait until you've both stopped performing for the camera.

06

Profile & Business Portrait

プロフィール・ビジネスポートレート

For your website, business cards and social media. A photo that looks like you on a good day, not like a stock image.

07

Photo Wedding

フォトウェディング

Before or after the ceremony, in traditional or western dress. I pick the background and the time of day to suit what you're wearing.

08

Ceremony

セレモニー (挙式当日)

From getting ready through the ceremony to the group photos. We go through the running order on a call beforehand.

09

Business, Product & Food

ビジネス (店舗・商品・料理)

Half a day of interiors, products and dishes for your site, menu and social. We can start from what you want the photos to say.

10

Event & Corporate

イベント・法人

Seminars, mixers and in-house events, shot from where I won't get in the way of the running order.

11

Aviation & Landscape

航空機・風景の撮影代行

Aircraft at Haneda or Narita, and landscape work. Both are available on request.
