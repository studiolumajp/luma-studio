Sho's note

Former Hokkaido Government Office

Finished in 1888 with about 2.5 million bricks. It reopened on 25 July 2025 after restoration from 2019 to 2025, and it's 300 yen to go in.

Sho's note

Odori Park and the TV Tower

About 1.5 kilometres of flowerbeds, lawns and fountains, from West 1 to West 12. The TV Tower deck at the east end is 1,200 yen.

Sho's note

JR Tower Observatory T38

The deck is 160 metres up, at Sapporo Station. It's open until 10:00 pm (last entry 9:30 pm) and costs 740 yen.

We meet at the South Exit of Sapporo Station, and I'll confirm the exact spot with you before the day. The Clock Tower is about 10 minutes on foot from the station, and about 5 from Odori Station.

South of the park, Tanuki-koji is a fully covered arcade of about 200 shops, around 900 metres long across seven blocks. It dates from 1873. Past it is Susukino, the city's entertainment district.

If it rains or snows, we go underground. Chi-Ka-Ho, a walkway of about 520 metres that opened in 2011, links Sapporo Station and Odori Station.

FAQ

Questions people ask

Can we do this in winter?

Yes. On snowy days we use Chi-Ka-Ho, the underground walkway between Sapporo Station and Odori Station. The Sapporo Snow Festival 2027 runs from 4 to 11 February 2027, at Odori, Susukino and Tsudome.

When do the plums bloom at Hiraoka Park?

Usually in early to mid May, but it varies by year. It's a spring-only option, so I'll check how the bloom is going before your date.
