From your guide

What I point out on the way

I spent nine years in Brisbane, from junior high to the end of university, and I guide in English or Japanese. I check our food stops with each shop before the day.

Sho's note

Chureito Pagoda

It's 398 steps up to the deck, about 25 minutes' walk from Shimoyoshida Station. The city warns it gets very busy.

Sho's note

Kitaguchi Hongu Fuji Sengen Shrine

More than 1,900 years of history, one of Japan's largest wooden torii and a cedar about 1,000 years old. Its fire festival is on 26 and 27 August.

Sho's note

Mt Fuji Panoramic Ropeway

About three minutes up to a deck at 1,075 metres. The return fare rises from 1,000 yen to 1,200 yen on 1 November 2026.

Fujiyoshida sits high up on volcanic soil that was poor for rice, so flour-based food took hold. That's why lunch here is Yoshida udon, known for its firm bite.

Cloud comes and goes, so I check the live cameras on the morning and move the route around if the mountain is hidden.

FAQ

Questions people ask

Will we definitely see Mt Fuji?

Nobody can promise that. On a grey day we lean on the art museum, the free Mt Fuji World Heritage Centre and the cedar forest at the shrine.

Can we skip the stairs?

Yes. We'll leave out the pagoda steps and spend longer at Honcho-dori, Kanadorii and the shrine.
