From your guide

What I point out on the way

I spent nine years in Brisbane, from junior high to the end of university, and I guide in English or Japanese. I check our food stops with each shop before the day.

Sho's note

Kenrokuen

Entry is free in the early morning, before the regular opening. After that it's 320 yen.

Sho's note

Shima

A teahouse built in 1820 and an Important Cultural Property. It's 500 yen to go in.

Sho's note

Omicho Market

About 170 shops. Many close on Wednesdays, so I plan lunch around that.

We meet at the Tsuzumi-mon gate outside Kanazawa Station, 13.7 metres tall and modelled on the Noh hand drum. Kanazawa gets a lot of rain and snow, and the dome behind the gate was designed as an umbrella held out to arriving passengers.

Gold leaf here is beaten to a ten-thousandth of a millimetre, and Kinkaku-ji in Kyoto uses about 200,000 sheets of it.

FAQ

Questions people ask

When is Kenrokuen quietest?

I don't have hard numbers, but the free early-morning opening may be quieter. If you're up early, we can start there.

Why is there no two-day route?

One full day covers the old town well. I can stretch it to two if you ask, but the second day would be light.
