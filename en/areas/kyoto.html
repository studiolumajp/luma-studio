From your guide

What I point out on the way

I spent nine years in Brisbane, from junior high to the end of university, and I guide in English or Japanese. I check our food stops with each shop before the day.

Sho's note

Fushimi Inari Taisha

Right outside JR Inari Station. It never closes and entry is free. The full mountain loop is about 4 km and two hours.

Sho's note

Nishiki Market

390 metres of food shops with 400 years behind them. Eating while walking isn't allowed, so we eat in or just in front of each shop.

Sho's note

Kinkaku-ji

It burned down in 1950 and was rebuilt in 1955. About 200,000 sheets of Kanazawa gold leaf went into it.

Kyoto City has asked visitors to spread out around Fushimi Inari because of crowding, and the Arashiyama bamboo grove is quietest around 8 am. I set start times with that in mind.

Nijo Castle was built in 1603, and in 1867 the return of power to the emperor was announced there.

FAQ

Questions people ask

How early can we start at Fushimi Inari?

The shrine never closes, so as early as you like. I'll suggest a time when we plan the day.

Is Kinkaku-ji on the one-day route?

No, the full day is already busy. It's on day two, after Arashiyama.
