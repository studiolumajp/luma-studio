What I point out on the way

I spent nine years in Brisbane, from junior high to the end of university, and I guide in English or Japanese. I check our food stops with each shop before the day.

Sho's note

Kushida Shrine

A 13-metre Yamakasa float stands here all year except June, next to a ginkgo said to be 1,000 years old.

Sho's note

Umegae mochi

There's no plum inside, just rice-cake dough around sweet red bean paste. It's 150 yen each, from more than 30 shops on the approach.

Sho's note

Kyushu National Museum

Japan's fourth national museum, 10 minutes' walk from Dazaifu Station. It's 700 yen and closed on Mondays.

Tocho-ji is said to have been founded in 806 by Kobo Daishi. Its Buddha is 10.8 metres tall and took from 1988 to 1992 to carve.

Fukuoka had 103 licensed yatai at the end of January 2024, about 90% of Japan's street stalls. We go for the food.

FAQ

Questions people ask

Why is it called umegae mochi if there's no plum?

The story goes that after Michizane was sent to Dazaifu, an old woman brought him mochi on a plum branch.

Is the yatai dinner a drinking tour?

No. We go to a yatai for the food, not a bar crawl.
