About the founder

The person behind the camera

I grew up in Aichi and live in Yokohama now, with nine years in Brisbane, Australia in between. I work in English and Japanese, and I handle all four services myself.

"Change where you are and you change who you become."

I moved to Australia at 15 and stayed for nine years. Back in Japan I kept three things going at once: teaching English, taking photos and organising events. Since 2022 I've hosted S.League, a health mahjong community that's now up to 24 events and 367 people who have taken part.

The thread running through all of it is confidence. I try to build it with words, and with time spent alongside other people. Whatever you book, it gets made for you and nobody else.
