When to book LUMA Path

When you can't be with family or friends every day

I can cover the days you can't. On the days you're free, you're welcome to come along. You can chat with me in Japanese as we go. Pick from 17 areas, including Asakusa, Kamakura and Kyoto. Each area has its own English page, so you can send the link straight to your guests.

When colleagues or clients have time between meetings

On the day they land, or in a gap before their flight home, I can show them around one neighbourhood in about 2 hours. I guide one group at a time, up to six people. I left Japan for Brisbane in January 2011, in my last year of junior high, and stayed for nine years. I'll do all the explaining in English, so you don't have to.

Who runs LUMA

"Change where you are and you change who you become."

I was born in Aichi, and at 15 I moved to Brisbane, Australia, where I lived for nine years. I spent my formative years there, from junior high school through to university graduation, and I also worked at a university in Brisbane. My strength is understanding other cultures and bringing people from different backgrounds together. Since coming home to Japan in 2020, I've put my Japanese and English to work in a wide range of settings.
