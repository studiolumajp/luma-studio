About — the founder

Founder of LUMA

Born in Aichi, based in Yokohama. A 9-year resident of Brisbane, Australia. Bilingual EN/JA. All four services are delivered personally by the founder.

"A change of environment changes a life."

I moved to Australia at 15 and lived there for nine years. Back in Japan I built parallel practices — English education, photography, and community organising. Since 2022, I've hosted "S.League," a health-mahjong community that has grown to 24 events and 367 cumulative participants over four years.

My theme: support self-confidence with words and shared experience. Every service is built one-on-one, around you.
