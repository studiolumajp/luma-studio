When to book LUMA Path

When you can't be with family or friends every day

I can cover the days you can't. On the days you're free, you're welcome to come along. You can chat with me in Japanese as we go. Pick from 16 areas, including Asakusa, Kamakura and Kyoto. Each area has its own English page, so you can send the link straight to your guests.

When colleagues or clients have time between meetings

On the day they land, or before their flight home, a half day of 3 to 4 hours covers one neighbourhood at an easy pace. I guide one group at a time, up to six people.
