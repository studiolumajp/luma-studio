How it works

How each project runs

Websites

  1. 01

    Discovery call

    We talk through your goals, your audience and how many pages you need.

  2. 02

    NDA, quote & contract

    We sign a non-disclosure agreement (NDA) first, then sign the contract once you're happy with the quote.

  3. 03

    Payment up front

    You pay the full fee within 7 days of the invoice date, and I start once payment is confirmed.

  4. 04

    Design

    We settle the structure and the look. Two rounds of revisions are included.

  5. 05

    Build

    Built on Cloudflare Pages, with an editor (microCMS) if you want to make updates yourself.

  6. 06

    Sign-off & launch

    You check everything works, and the site goes live.

  7. 07

    Website care

    If you'd like me to keep the site updated, care continues on a monthly or yearly plan.

Social media

  1. 01

    Review

    I look at your current account, your posts and who you want to reach.

  2. 02

    Access

    You add me to your business account as a team member.

  3. 03

    Setup

    Bilingual profile, posting guidelines and your first nine posts.

  4. 04

    Monthly posting

    You approve the monthly calendar, then I schedule the posts.

  5. 05

    Monthly report

    On the Standard plan, you get a summary of the month's results.

iOS apps

  1. 01

    Requirements

    We decide the features, and I explain App Store review requirements up front.

  2. 02

    Prototype

    I build the core screens and share them with you through TestFlight.

  3. 03

    Full build

    I build the release version based on what the prototype showed us.

  4. 04

    Review & launch

    We submit the app from your Apple Developer account and publish it.

  5. 05

    Maintenance

    iOS updates and minor fixes on an annual plan.
