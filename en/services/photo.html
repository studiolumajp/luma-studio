Details

  • Delivery Photos from every category reach you within 7 days of the shoot. If you need them sooner, add speed delivery and you'll have them within 4 days.
  • Extra time The half-day plans cover 3 to 4 hours. Anything longer is charged as extra shooting time at +¥11,000 per hour.
  • Venue fees For indoor shoots, you cover any studio or rental-space fee. If you don't have a venue yet, I'll suggest a few.
  • Quotes Once I know what you'd like, I'll send a free quote with every cost listed. You pay nothing at that stage.
  • Payment The fee needs to be paid in full in advance, and your date is confirmed when the payment clears. Businesses can ask for an invoice.
  • Travel I charge travel at cost, and only for shoots outside Tokyo's 23 wards, Yokohama and Kawasaki.
  • Cancellation The full terms are in the cancellation policy.

How it works

From your first message to the finished photos

It takes four steps, and I come to you for the shoot.

01
Step 01

Tell me what you have in mind

What's the occasion, where and when would you like to shoot, and how many people will be there? Let me know what the photos are for, too.

02
Step 02

Location and plan

I'll suggest a few spots, give you some ideas on what to wear and sketch out the day. When you accept the quote, I'll send a payment link. Your date is confirmed once the payment clears.

03
Step 03

The shoot

There's no rush. We talk as we go, and I start shooting once everyone has relaxed. You can check the shots on the camera along the way.

04
Step 04

Your photos

I choose and finish the photos, and they reach you within 7 days of the shoot.
