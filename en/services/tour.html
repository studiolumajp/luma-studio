Japan with a local who spent nine years in Australia

For you

A private guide for your days in Japan

I guide one group at a time. These tours suit you if:

  • It's your first time in Japan, and you'd like someone to ask about everything from ticket machines to menus.
  • You're travelling with children or parents, and want a day planned around them.
  • You've just landed, or fly home tomorrow, and have a few hours free.
  • A friend or colleague in Japan is booking a day for you. They're welcome to come along.
  • You want to get beyond Tokyo, to Kyoto, Mt Fuji, Kanazawa or the Izu coast.

Your guide

I'm Sho, and I'll be your guide

In January 2011, in my last year of junior high, I left Japan for Brisbane. I stayed until I'd finished university in March 2020. That's nine years of Australian school, uni and part-time jobs.

I speak English and Japanese without any trouble, and I don't get shy around people from other cultures. What I care about most on the day is simple: that you're having a good time.

I'm based in Yokohama. I've been photographing planes since 2009. Since moving back to Japan in 2020, photography has taken me to airports and towns all over the country. Every area on this page is somewhere I've been myself.

I have a serious kiwi fruit allergy (anaphylaxis) and react to some fruit in the rose family. So I check our food stops with the restaurants before the day. If there's anything you can't eat, tell me when you first get in touch.

Tours run in English. If someone in your group is more comfortable in Japanese, I can switch.

Please note

How the day works

I look after the day itself. Train tickets, hotels and any entry tickets you want to book ahead are yours to arrange. I'll tell you exactly where and when to meet me.

We get around by train, bus and on foot, the same way people here do. I don't drive guests, and I'll show you how the ticket machines work as we go.
