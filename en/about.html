About me — Founder of LUMA

A change of environment
changes a life.

I moved to Brisbane at 15 and stayed nine years. Now based in Yokohama, I personally run all four LUMA services — English lessons, portrait photography, the S.League health-mahjong community, and English-guided Tokyo tours.

Profile

Founder of LUMA

Born in Aichi, based in Yokohama

I moved to Brisbane, Australia at the age of 15, at my mother's encouragement. Nine years in a place where I knew neither the language nor the culture taught me three things: how to express myself, how to let go of the fear of being wrong, and how to keep showing up for the people around me.

Back in Japan I built parallel practices — English coaching, photography, and community organising. Since 2022 I've hosted "S.League," a health mahjong community that has grown to 24 events and 367 cumulative participants in four years.

My theme is simple: support self-confidence with words and shared experience. As LUMA's founder, I personally run all four service areas.

🇦🇺 9 years in Australia 🎓 English coach 📸 Nikon Z6III shooter 🀄 Mahjong organiser
