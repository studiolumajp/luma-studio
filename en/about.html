About me – Founder of LUMA

Change where you are,
and you change too.

I moved to Brisbane at 15 and stayed nine years. I'm based in Yokohama now, and I run all four LUMA services myself: English lessons, portrait photography, the S.League health-mahjong community and English-guided Tokyo tours.

Profile

Founder of LUMA

Born in Aichi, based in Yokohama

Mum encouraged me to go, so at 15 I moved to Brisbane, Australia. I spent nine years there, in a place where I knew neither the language nor the culture. It taught me three things: how to say what I actually mean, how to stop being scared of getting it wrong and how to keep showing up for the people around me.

Back in Japan I started teaching English, taking photos and running events, all at once. Since 2022 I've hosted "S.League," a health mahjong community that has grown to 24 events and 367 cumulative participants in four years.

What I'm after is simple. I want people to feel surer of themselves, and I think words and time spent together are how that happens. All four LUMA services are mine to run, start to finish.

🇦🇺 9 years in Australia 🎓 English coach 📸 Nikon Z6III shooter 🀄 Mahjong organiser
