Section 02

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 October 2026

This policy explains how LUMA collects, uses and protects your personal information. It follows Japan's Act on the Protection of Personal Information and the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

1. Who we are

The controller of your personal information is Sho Asano, trading as LUMA, based in Chiba Prefecture, Japan. We'll provide our full address without delay if you ask for it.

This policy covers studio-lumadesign.com and these services:

  • LUMA Frame (on-location photography)
  • LUMA Path (private English-language tours for visitors to Japan)
  • LUMA Craft (web, social media and app production)
  • S.League (health mahjong tournaments)

For anything about your personal information, contact us through the contact form or at info@studio-lumadesign.com.

2. Information we collect

  • Contact form: first name, last name, email address, phone number (optional), the service you're interested in, and your message.
  • Applications (Google Forms): your name (a nickname is fine), email address, phone number, and the information the service needs. For S.League, this includes your mahjong experience and how many times you've taken part.
  • Payments: Square processes card payments. We don't hold your card number.
  • Website visits: if you agree to analytics cookies, Google Analytics 4 cookies and data about how you use the site. Our hosting provider, Cloudflare, also processes your IP address and similar connection data when you visit.
  • LINE: if you contact us on LINE, your LINE account name and your messages.

3. Why we use it and our legal basis

Each use of your information relies on a legal basis under GDPR Article 6(1).

We don't share your personal information with anyone else, except with your consent or when the law requires it (for example, a request from the police or a court). We never sell your personal information.

5. How long we keep it

We keep your information until we've fulfilled the purpose we collected it for. After that, we keep only what we need to follow up on your enquiry, and what the law requires us to keep, such as accounting and tax records. Then we delete it.

If you've signed up for event emails, we keep your email address until you withdraw your consent.

6. Cookies and analytics

The only cookies we use are Google Analytics 4 cookies. They load only if you choose "Accept" on the banner shown on your first visit. If you choose "Decline", every part of the site still works.

7. Photos and video

Event staff may take photos and video at S.League events. These may appear on our website (studio-lumadesign.com) and on social media (Instagram, etc.). If you'd rather not be featured, say so in the notes field of the application form or contact us through the contact form.

For anyone under 18, we confirm a parent or guardian's consent before taking photos.

8. Your rights

Under GDPR Articles 15 to 22, you have the right to:

  • access the personal information we hold about you
  • have it corrected
  • have it erased
  • restrict how we process it
  • receive it in a portable format (data portability)
  • object to our processing of it
  • withdraw your consent at any time. This doesn't affect processing carried out before you withdrew it.

To make a request, contact us using the details in section 14. We may ask for more information to confirm your identity. We'll normally reply within one month. If a request is complex, we may extend this by up to two more months. We'll tell you, and explain why, within the first month. There's no charge, unless a request is clearly unfounded or excessive.

We accept requests under Japan's Act on the Protection of Personal Information (for disclosure and similar) through the same contact point.

9. Complaints

If you live in the EU, you can lodge a complaint with the data protection supervisory authority in the country where you live, where you work, or where you believe the breach took place (GDPR Article 77). The European Data Protection Board lists these authorities on its members page.

In Japan, you can contact the Personal Information Protection Commission.

10. Required information and automated decisions

To accept an application, we need your name, your contact details and the information the service requires. Without them, we can't take your application. Everything else is optional.

We don't make automated decisions about you, including profiling.

11. Security and data breaches

We protect your information with appropriate security measures, including controls on who can access it.

If a data breach occurs, we'll report it to the supervisory authority as the law requires. Under the GDPR, this is generally within 72 hours. Where required, we'll also tell you.

12. Children under 16

If you're under 16, you need a parent or guardian's consent before agreeing to event emails or analytics cookies (GDPR Article 8). Some EU countries set a lower age, down to 13.

13. Changes to this policy

We'll post any changes to this policy on this page. If we make a significant change, we'll point it out clearly on this page. The date at the top shows when the policy was last updated.

14. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, use the contact form or email info@studio-lumadesign.com.

You may reschedule up to twice. A third request is treated as a cancellation.

Please settle the new shoot date within 3 days of your request. If it is not settled within 3 days, that is treated as a cancellation. To allow time to prepare, the new date must also fall 7 or more days after the day you contact me (for example, if you write on 15 August, the new date must be 22 August or later).

Bad weather

1. Advance notice
For shoots planned outdoors, if the Japan Meteorological Agency's chance of rain for your shoot date and municipality is 50% or higher at 9:00 am four days before the shoot, I'll email you. Reply by 11:59 pm that same day to tell me whether you want to reschedule or cancel. No rescheduling or cancellation fee applies in that case, and a cancellation is refunded in full. If I don't hear from you by then, the shoot goes ahead as booked and the standard terms above apply from that point.

2. Cancellation on warnings and advisories
If a heavy rain warning, storm warning or strong wind advisory (or an emergency warning above any of these) is in force for the shoot location's municipality, including on the day itself, the shoot is cancelled. You may either reschedule or receive a full refund, and no rescheduling or cancellation fee applies.

3. Other bad weather
Where only a heavy rain advisory is in force, or the weather otherwise falls short of point 2, the standard terms apply. That said, if I judge the shoot unsafe or unworkable for the quality of the photos, I'll propose cancelling or moving it, and no rescheduling or cancellation fee applies in that case either.

Cancellation by the photographer

If the shoot cannot go ahead because of the photographer's sudden illness or injury, a major equipment failure, natural disaster, infectious disease or other force majeure, you may either reschedule or receive a full refund. No rescheduling or cancellation fee applies in that case. If you then cancel for your own reasons, the policy above applies.

Wedding day shoots

For shoots on the day of the ceremony (the Ceremony plan), the date cannot be moved, so the "Rescheduling" and "Bad weather" terms above do not apply. Only cancellation is possible, and refunds work as follows.

Reworks and refunds after delivery

If you'd like something changed about the finished photos, I'll rework them once, free of charge. Please get in touch within 14 days of delivery.

Only where a rework does not resolve a technical fault (a serious focus or exposure error, corrupted data and the like) will a reshoot or refund apply. Refunds cannot be given for differences in style or taste, or for the expressions, weather or surroundings on the day. You're welcome to look through sample work before the shoot, so please do raise any questions about the look you're after.

Travel and out-of-pocket costs

For shoots outside Yokohama, Kawasaki and Tokyo's 23 wards, and for any venue booked for an indoor shoot, costs already incurred at the point of cancellation (non-refundable transport bookings, for example) are charged separately from the cancellation fee. You will be told in advance when this applies.

3. Applies to all services

Refund methods

Card payments are refunded to the original card via Square (refunds typically take 3 to 10 business days). Bank-transfer payments are refunded by transfer to your specified account. Processing fees are covered by LUMA, so they never come out of your refund. Where this policy says "full refund", you get back everything you paid.

How to get in touch

Send cancellations and rescheduling requests via the contact form or email (info@studio-lumadesign.com). Timing is measured from when your message is received.

Part 1 applies to all S.League tournaments, part 2 to photography sessions (LUMA Frame) and part 3 to both. Completing your booking is taken as agreement to the applicable policy.
