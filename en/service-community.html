Overview

Building places where people connect.

A turning point isn't a moment.
It's a place to belong.

Growing up, I never really had a place where I fit. Finding one changed that. It's also where "S.League" came from, a health mahjong community I started in 2022. It's 24 events in now, with 367 people through the door. LUMA Circle takes what I learned there past mahjong, into social meetups, one-off events and any other "place to belong" you'd like to build with me.

Why work with us

  • 🀄 I've run 24 mahjong tournaments, so I know how a day like this holds together
  • 🌐 I can run the whole thing in English, Japanese or both
  • 🤝 I'll help you plan it, then run it on the day
  • 🎁 Everyone goes home with a gift, wherever they finish

Flagship Event

S.League – four years and counting

The flagship community inside LUMA Circle. The motto is "earnest fun".

Since 2022

Real mahjong, in a healthy way.

I started S.League in July 2022. Each session brings 16–20 players together for genuinely competitive games, without alcohol, smoking or betting. Pros and people who only picked up the game last month sit at the same tables. Everyone takes home a parting gift.
