Shooting notes: Two lenses for one harbour

Shooting Notes

Where to shoot in Yokohama

Where you stand and when you go changes the picture completely.

Red Brick and Shinko Pier

The plaza sits right on the water, so both wide and close work. On days when a liner is berthed, lining it up with the Bay Bridge says "port" immediately.

The skyline at dusk

About thirty minutes before sunset the Landmark Tower and the Ferris wheel settle into silhouette. Stand on the water side and face west.

Winter nights

The wheel and the city illuminations overlap for a few weeks. On New Year's Eve the plaza empties out enough to shoot it with no one in frame.
