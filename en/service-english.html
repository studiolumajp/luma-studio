Overview

Real English, built together at your pace.

Not "correct" English.
English that lands, and stays.

I lived in Australia from age 15 to 24. I was vice president of a Japanese students association looking after 500+ students, and I tutored English the whole time I was there. So I don't teach "correct" English in the abstract. What I'm after is your confidence in a real conversation. Online or in person, either works just as well.

Background

  • 🇦🇺 9 years living in Brisbane, Australia (age 15–24)
  • 🎓 Vice president of a 500+ student association
  • 🌐 Bilingual EN / JA, so we can switch languages when it helps
  • 🗣️ Taught English right through those nine years, from beginners to exam prep
What's Included

Five things I teach, and they all come back to speaking

Mix them however you like. Most people start with two.

01

Conversational English

日常英会話

We talk about travel, everyday life and whatever you're into, until speaking stops feeling like hard work. Beginners welcome.

02

IELTS / TOEIC Prep

試験対策

We go through the test section by section, work out where the marks are, then run mock papers. The idea is to get you to your target score without wasting months on the wrong things.

03

Business English

ビジネス英語

Emails, meetings and presentations, in the words people actually use at work. Useful if you're on a global team or thinking about a move overseas.

04

Pronunciation & Listening

発音・リスニング

We work on sounds people understand the first time you say them, and on keeping up when someone talks at full speed.

05

Study Abroad Advice

留学サポート

Picking a school, settling in, getting your head around living somewhere new. I did all of it myself over 9 years in Australia.
