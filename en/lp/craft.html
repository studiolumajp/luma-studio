JA / Top page headline

ノーレートでも勝ち負けのある、本気の麻雀。

EN / Top page headline

Serious mahjong, with nothing at stake but the result.

Both headlines introduce S.League, a health mahjong tournament in Shimbashi, Tokyo. The Japanese uses 「ノーレート」, a mahjong term that makes no sense in a literal English version, so the English headline says in one line that the only thing at stake is the result.
