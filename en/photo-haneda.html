LUMA Frame · Photography

Haneda, from the deck and from below.

121 frames from Tokyo International Airport (Haneda) — shot from the observation decks and from Keihinjima Tsubasa Park, where arriving aircraft pass directly overhead. The full body of work is in the portfolio; there are separate pages for Itami and Shonan and Enoshima.

Shooting notes: Three winter visits to the decks / Haneda after dark / Keihinjima Tsubasa Park

Shooting Notes

Two places to shoot at Haneda

An observation deck above the apron, and a park directly under the approach path. The same airport gives you completely different pictures.

The observation decks

Looking down on the apron lets you frame a whole aircraft together with the scale of the airport. Terminals 1 and 2 face west, so on a clear evening Mount Fuji appears behind the buildings.

Keihinjima Tsubasa Park

Arriving aircraft pass directly overhead. The view up at an aircraft's underside exists only here. A promenade runs along the water, with the terminals visible across the channel.

After the sun goes down

Haneda has a long night. Taxiway lights and terminal windows carry the frame well past sunset, and panning turns those lights into streaks.

Commissions — aviation shoots at Haneda, Narita or Itami start at ¥22,000 (tax incl.) for half a day. Tell me the aircraft or the time of day you want and we build the plan from there.
