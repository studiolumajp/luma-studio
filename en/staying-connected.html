Staying connected in Japan

When you land in Japan, you'll want data on your phone straight away. At some point you'll probably also join public Wi-Fi at your hotel or a café. Here are two services for those two situations, with the details you'll want before you buy.

Saily: an eSIM for mobile data in Japan

Saily is an eSIM service from the company that made NordVPN. You set up the eSIM in the Saily app before you leave, and your plan starts automatically when you arrive. You won't need to queue up to buy a SIM card at an airport or a station.

  • Plans range from 1 GB to unlimited data.
  • Saily works in more than 200 countries and regions.
  • You'll know what your data costs before you leave, so you can avoid expensive roaming charges.
  • Saily provides your mobile data, and you keep your usual number for calls and texts (SMS).
  • You can add a plan for your next trip to the same eSIM, so there's no need to install a new one each time.
  • If your phone turns out not to support eSIM, you get a full refund.
  • Chat support is available 24 hours a day.

NordVPN encrypts your internet traffic and hides your IP address. It's useful whenever you join public Wi-Fi at a hotel, a station or a café, in Japan or back home. NordVPN's Black Friday sale runs until 9 December 2026.
