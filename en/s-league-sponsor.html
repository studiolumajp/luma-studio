Why S.League

Small in scale, but you're in the room all day.

Our strength is density rather than scale. Each event puts 16〜20 players in one room, from half a day (about 6 hours) to a full day (about 10 hours). Your name isn't an ad they scroll past. It reads as a company backing something they turn up for.

Face-to-face reach

Every event runs with 16〜20 players in the room, from half a day (about 6 hours) to a full day (about 10 hours). Your signage, prizes and samples sit in front of them the whole time.

Public all-time rankings

We publish the all-time records of 126 players on our official site. Players come back to it to check where they sit. Your logo can sit there too.

Professional event photos

I shoot the events myself: portrait photography is the other half of my work. You're welcome to use the photos with your logo in them for your own marketing.

Post-event report

After each event we send through a report: how many turned up, the age mix, where your name appeared and the photos. You can see exactly what the sponsorship did.

How it works

From enquiry to your first event.

1

Enquiry

Send us a message with 'sponsorship' in it. That's all we need to send our media kit back.

2

Consultation

We can meet online or near Shimbashi, and settle on the plan and where your name goes.

3

Application

We sign a short agreement, then send the invoice. Once payment lands, we start getting things ready.

4

Event day

Your name goes up on the site and into the announcements. On the day it's on the venue signage, and we say it out loud.

5

Report

We send through the exposure results and the photos. Decide about the next one once you've seen them.
