S.League — Sponsorship

Help us grow S.League.

S.League is a no-gambling, no-alcohol, no-smoking "healthy mahjong" community that has run in Shimbashi, Tokyo since 2022 — 24 events and 367 cumulative participants, from first-timers to professional players. We are looking for companies and local businesses to sponsor our tournaments.

Why S.League

Small community, guaranteed reach.

S.League's strength is density, not scale. Each event brings 18-24 participants together for about 10 hours, so your brand is remembered not as an ad, but as a supporter of the community.

🤝

Face-to-face reach

18-24 participants spend around 10 hours at each event. On-site logos, prizes, and sampling reach every single one of them.

📊

Public all-time rankings

The cumulative results of 126 players are published on our official site — a page participants revisit again and again. Your logo can live there.

📷

Professional event photos

The organiser is also a portrait photographer. Event photos featuring your logo are available for your own marketing.

📮

Post-event report

After every event we deliver a report covering attendance, demographics, exposure, and photos — so you can see what your sponsorship achieved.

Sponsorship plans

Four plans to choose from.

In-kind sponsor

¥5,000+ worth of goods

  • Provide prizes or product samples
  • Name announced at the awards ceremony
  • Credit in the event report
  • Photo feature in our results post on X

Event sponsor

¥10,000 / event

  • Logo and link on the event page
  • Logo panel and flyers at the venue
  • Mentions in two posts on X
  • Event photos featuring your logo
Recommended · one per event

Title sponsor

¥50,000 / event

First-time sponsors: ¥40,000

  • Your name in the event title, on all announcements
  • Announcements and a greeting slot at the event
  • Permanent logo on our site and archive
  • Sampling and flyers to all participants
  • Reuse licence for photos and reports

Annual partner

¥120,000 / year (approx. 6 events)

Premium plan ¥150,000 available

  • Title-level exposure at every event
  • Permanent logo on the all-time rankings page
  • Year-round reuse licence for photos and reports
  • Ongoing coupon and offer distribution to participants
  • Premium plan includes title rights for two events

All prices include tax. The ¥40,000 first-time rate applies to companies sponsoring S.League for the first time.
Reuse licence = permission to use event photos and reports featuring your logo in your own website, social media, in-store, and print marketing. Terms and duration are provided on application.
We may decline sponsorships that conflict with the healthy-mahjong ethos, such as gambling-related products or products unsuitable for under-20s.

How it works

From enquiry to your first event.

1

Enquiry

Contact us with "sponsorship" in your message. We will send you our media kit.

2

Consultation

We discuss plans and placements online or near Shimbashi.

3

Application

We exchange a simple agreement and send an invoice. Placement starts after payment.

4

Event day

Site placement and announcements go live. On the day: venue signage and announcements.

5

Report

You receive a post-event report with exposure results and photos, plus renewal options.
