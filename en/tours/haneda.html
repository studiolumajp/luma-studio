Overview

An airport worth a day of its own

Haneda has four runways and runs 24 hours. The wind decides how they're used: with a south wind all four are in use, and with a north wind three. Either way, up to 80 take-offs and landings an hour are possible.

All three terminals have free observation decks. The decks at Terminals 1 and 2 are open 6:30 am to 10 pm, and the Terminal 3 deck is open 24 hours. Terminal 2 also has FLIGHT DECK TOKYO, an indoor viewing floor, open 5 am to 11 pm.

The airport opened as Tokyo Airport in 1931. Near Tenkubashi Station stands the Haneda Peace Torii, first given to the old Anamori Inari Shrine in 1929. In September 1945 about 3,000 local people were ordered to leave within 48 hours. The torii stayed on airport land until it was moved to its present spot in 1999.

Highlights

  • Watch planes from the free decks at all three terminals
  • Stay dry at FLIGHT DECK TOKYO, the indoor viewing floor in Terminal 2
  • Look for Mt Fuji from Terminal 1 on clear, cool mornings
  • Visit the Haneda Peace Torii and hear how the airport grew
  • Walk Edo Koji, the old-Edo-style street of shops in Terminal 3
  • Add a hangar tour you've booked (half and full day)

Good to know

  • The observation decks and FLIGHT DECK TOKYO are free. The outdoor decks can close for weather or security reasons.
  • The ANA Blue Hangar Tour is free and takes about 1 hour 15 minutes, Tuesday to Saturday. Online booking opens at 9:30 am, 30 days ahead, and there's no waiting list. I'll tell you when booking opens and plan the day around your slot.
  • The English page for the ANA Blue Base Tour says the tour is guided in Japanese only. I haven't been able to confirm English guiding for the ANA Blue Hangar Tour or JAL SKY MUSEUM.
  • All three hangar and museum tours are for primary-school age and up.
  • Photos aren't allowed at security screening, immigration, customs or quarantine. Drones and kites aren't allowed around the airport.
  • Rain plan: FLIGHT DECK TOKYO, the hangar tours and Edo Koji are all indoors.
  • I look after the day itself. Train tickets, hotels and any entry tickets you want to book ahead are yours to arrange. I'll tell you exactly where and when to meet me.
  • We get around by train, bus and on foot, the same way people here do. I don't drive guests, and I'll show you how the ticket machines work as we go.

FAQ

Questions people ask

Can we see Mt Fuji?

Sometimes. The government's airport office lists the Terminal 1 6th-floor and rooftop decks and the Terminal 3 5th-floor deck as Fuji viewpoints. Clear, cool days are best, especially in the morning.

Which runway will we see?

That depends on the wind on the day, so I'll explain it on the deck rather than promise it in advance.
