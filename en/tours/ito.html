Ito.

Ito, where an Englishman built Japan's first Western-style ship

A seaside town on the east side of the Izu Peninsula, with a 1928 wooden inn by the river and a volcano you can ride a lift up. Up to six people, by bus, train and on foot.

Overview

A story English speakers already half know

William Adams, the English navigator known in Japan as Miura Anjin, reached Japan in 1600. In Ito he built Japan's first Western-style sailing ship, about 80 tonnes, finished in 1604, and then a 120-tonne ship, the San Buena Ventura, in 1607.

Tokaikan, a wooden hot-spring inn built in 1928, is now open to visitors as a cultural building, seven minutes' walk from Ito Station. There's a small café inside, but no bathing.

On a full day we take the bus out to Mt Omuro, a 580-metre scoria cone formed about 4,000 years ago. A lift runs to the top, and the walk around the crater rim is about a kilometre.

Highlights

  • Walk through Tokaikan, a wooden inn from 1928
  • Hear how William Adams built ships in Ito
  • Ride the lift up Mt Omuro and walk the crater rim
  • Cross the Kadowaki suspension bridge on the Jogasaki coast (full day, time permitting)
  • Eat dried fish or kinmedai at Ito Marine Town

Full day (7 to 8 hours)

The town in the morning, the volcano in the afternoon.

  1. Tokaikan, the river path and Anjin Memorial Parkthe morning on foot.
  2. Lunchdried fish or kinmedai.
  3. Mt Omurobus from Ito Station to the end of the line, the lift (1,000 yen return), then the rim walk of about 1,000 metres, 20 to 30 minutes.
  4. Jogasaki coast, if time allowsIzukyu train to Jogasaki-Kaigan Station (650 yen), then about 35 minutes' walk to the Kadowaki suspension bridge, 48 metres long and about 23 metres high.

The Jogasaki coast was formed by lava from the same eruption of Mt Omuro.

Good to know

  • The Mt Omuro lift stops for lightning and strong gusts. If it isn't running, we'll swap in Ito Marine Town or more time in town.
  • The lift runs 9 am to 5 pm from March to September and 9 am to 4 pm from October to February.
  • The Jogasaki coast path runs over rocky shoreline. Wear shoes with good grip.
  • Tokaikan isn't wheelchair accessible. Tell me if anyone in your group needs a step-free day.
  • Rain plan: Tokaikan and Ito Marine Town are both indoors.
  • I look after the day itself. Train tickets, hotels and any entry tickets you want to book ahead are yours to arrange. I'll tell you exactly where and when to meet me.
  • We get around by train, bus and on foot, the same way people here do. I don't drive guests, and I'll show you how the ticket machines work as we go.

FAQ

Questions people ask

Who was William Adams?

An English navigator, born on 24 September 1564, who reached Japan in 1600 and became known as Miura Anjin. In Ito he built Japan's first Western-style sailing ship.

Can we bathe at Tokaikan?

No. It's open for looking around, not for bathing. Ito Marine Town has a day-use hot spring if you'd like a soak afterwards.
