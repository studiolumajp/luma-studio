Overview

The mountain from the town side

Most people see Mt Fuji from a lookout. In Fujiyoshida you also see it at the end of an ordinary shopping street. Honcho-dori is five minutes' walk from Shimoyoshida Station, and the mountain sits right behind the old shop signs and power lines.

From the same station it's about 25 minutes on foot to Arakurayama Sengen Park, then 398 steps up to the deck by the Chureito Pagoda. The city itself warns that it gets very busy with overseas visitors, so I like to get there early.

Lunch is Yoshida udon. The town sits high up on volcanic soil that was poor for rice, so flour-based food took hold, and the noodles here are known for their firm bite.

Highlights

  • Climb the 398 steps to the Chureito Pagoda deck
  • See Mt Fuji rise behind the shopfronts of Honcho-dori
  • Walk the cedar-lined approach to Kitaguchi Hongu Fuji Sengen Shrine
  • Eat Yoshida udon, the thick, firm local noodle
  • Ride the Mt Fuji Panoramic Ropeway up to 1,075 metres (full day)
Routes

Pick the length that suits your day

2 hours

A walking loop from Shimoyoshida Station: the pagoda, then the old shopping street.

  1. Shimoyoshida Stationwe meet at the station.
  2. Chureito Pagodaabout 25 minutes on foot, then 398 steps to the viewing deck.
  3. Honcho-dorithe shopping street with Mt Fuji at the far end, five minutes from the station.

All on foot. The steps are the hard part.

Half day (3 to 4 hours)

Fujiyoshida on foot, with a short train ride to lunch and the shrine.

  1. Chureito Pagodathe view over the pagoda to the mountain.
  2. Honcho-dori and Kanadoriia torii gate that frames Mt Fuji and marks the way into the mountain's sacred ground.
  3. Fujikyu Line to Mt Fuji Stationa short ride, 230 yen.
  4. Yoshida udontoppings often include horse meat, and I'll help you order without it if you'd rather.
  5. Kitaguchi Hongu Fuji Sengen Shrine20 minutes' walk from Mt Fuji Station, with one of Japan's largest wooden torii and a cedar about 1,000 years old.

We finish at Mt Fuji Station.

Full day (7 to 8 hours)

Lake Kawaguchi in the morning, Fujiyoshida in the afternoon.

  1. Mt Fuji Panoramic Ropewayabout three minutes up to a deck at 1,075 metres.
  2. Oishi Parka 350-metre flower walk by the lake, with lavender from late June to mid-July and kochia in autumn.
  3. Itchiku Kubota Art Museumabout 20 minutes from Kawaguchiko Station on the Red Line bus.
  4. Chureito Pagoda and Honcho-doriback by Fujikyu train to Shimoyoshida for the afternoon view.

The Kawaguchiko sightseeing buses, including the Red Line, have a 1,500-yen day pass.

Two days

Fujiyoshida on day one, the lake and Oshino Hakkai on day two.

  1. Day 1Chureito Pagoda, Honcho-dori, Kitaguchi Hongu Fuji Sengen Shrine and Yoshida udon.
  2. Day 2the ropeway, Oishi Park and the Itchiku Kubota Art Museum, then a bus from Mt Fuji Station to Oshino Hakkai, eight spring-fed ponds listed as a Natural Monument.

Suits you if you're staying the night near the lake.

Good to know

  • The Chureito Pagoda deck is 398 steps up. If stairs are hard for you, tell me and I'll build the day around Honcho-dori and the shrine instead.
  • Clouds can hide Mt Fuji, and I can't promise a view. On a grey day we'll lean on the art museum, the free Mt Fuji World Heritage Centre and the cedar forest at the shrine.
  • The ropeway can stop in strong wind. It also runs shorter hours on 21 and 22 October and 25 and 26 November 2026 for inspections.
  • I look after the day itself. Train tickets, hotels and any entry tickets you want to book ahead are yours to arrange. I'll tell you exactly where and when to meet me.
  • We get around by train, bus and on foot, the same way people here do. I don't drive guests, and I'll show you how the ticket machines work as we go.
