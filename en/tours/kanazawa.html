Kanazawa, a castle town dressed in gold leaf

Kanazawa makes about 99% of Japan's gold leaf. We walk its garden, teahouse streets and market, by bus and on foot. Up to six people.

Overview

Gold, gardens and teahouses

We meet under the Tsuzumi-mon gate at Kanazawa Station. It's 13.7 metres tall and modelled on the hand drum used in Noh. Kanazawa gets a lot of rain and snow, and the glass dome behind the gate was designed as an umbrella held out to people arriving by train.

Kanazawa produces about 99% of Japan's gold leaf, beaten to a ten-thousandth of a millimetre. Kinkaku-ji in Kyoto uses about 200,000 sheets of it.

In the Higashi Chaya district, the teahouse Shima has stood since 1820 and is a nationally designated Important Cultural Property. You can go inside.

Highlights

  • Walk Kenrokuen garden, with free early-morning entry before the regular opening
  • Step inside Shima, a teahouse built in 1820
  • Have lunch at Omicho Market, home to about 170 shops
  • See the garden at the Nomura samurai house (full day)
  • Hear how Kanazawa's gold leaf ended up on Kinkaku-ji
Routes

Pick the length that suits your day

2 hours

The market and the teahouse district, by bus.

  1. Omicho Marketabout 5 minutes by bus to the Musashigatsuji-Omicho Market stop.
  2. Higashi Chaya district and Shimaby bus to Hashiba-cho, then 6 minutes on foot.

We finish at Hashiba-cho, with buses back to the station.

Half day (3 to 4 hours)

Garden and castle, lunch at the market, then the teahouses.

  1. Kenrokuenabout 12 minutes by bus, then a 5-minute walk.
  2. Kanazawa Castle Parkfree to walk in, next to the garden.
  3. Omicho Marketlunch.
  4. Higashi Chaya district.

Full day (7 to 8 hours)

The whole old town, with a samurai house and a modern art museum.

  1. Omicho Market12 minutes on foot from the station.
  2. Kenrokuen and Kanazawa Castle Park.
  3. 21st Century Museum of Contemporary Artthe free zone is open 9 am to 10 pm.
  4. Nomura samurai house5 minutes from the Korinbo bus stop, with a garden given two stars in the 2009 Michelin Green Guide Japon.
  5. Higashi Chaya and Shimato finish.

A one-day city bus pass costs 800 yen.

Two days

Kanazawa works best as one full day.

Two days are available on request, but the second day would be much lighter than the first.
